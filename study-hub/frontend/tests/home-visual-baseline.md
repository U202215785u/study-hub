## Design Language

- Canvas: `#10140F`; shell: `#151814`; surface: `#1B1D1A`; raised surface: `#252824`.
- Primary action: `#D7FF63` with `#11140F` action text. Content accents remain independent: purple `#8B73FF`, orange `#EA4E00`.
- Strong text: `#F5F6EE`; default text: `#D9DDCF`; muted text: `#8B9186`.
- Widget radius: `22px`; compact controls: `6px` to `10px`; borders use the 12% and 20% text-white alpha tokens.
- Typography is compact and work-focused: 40px dashboard title on desktop, 30px on mobile, 13px to 15px widget headings, 11px to 13px metadata.
- Primary navigation is one labeled landmark. At compact widths secondary routes move into the `更多` menu; the Dock becomes a trigger from 1024px to 1279px.
